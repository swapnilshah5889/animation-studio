# Motion choice
> Which motion fits which story beat, and what each one looks like from the front.

**When to use:** every time a plan shot says what a character "does".

**How:** match the beat to the motion. Arrival or travel: `walk_cycle` plus `move_character` over the
same seconds (the feet then match the distance), or `sneak` for quiet. Greeting: `wave` (`side` is the
character's own side; the arm toward the other character looks natural). Agreement: `nod`. Not knowing:
`shrug`. Wondering: `think` or `head_tilt`. Noticing something: `look_around`. Delight: `jump`, `clap`,
`laugh` (pair `laugh` with a happy or excited expression). Showing an object: `point` with a target in
frame fractions. Politeness: `bow`. While someone talks: `talk_gesture` on the speaker only.

**What fails and why:** the figures are flat cut-outs. Front view cannot turn the head or pitch the
body, so `bow` and `look_around` are small shifts, not real turns: use them as accents, not as the
shot's whole idea. `walk_cycle` without `move_character` marches in place. Two big motions on one arm
at once: the later one wins. Motions that are not in the capability list do not exist: never invent one.
