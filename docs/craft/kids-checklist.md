# Kids checklist
> Age-appropriate for 4 to 6: gentle, clear, safe. Checked by code too, so do not argue with a refusal.

**When to use:** writing the story, the lines and every description.

**How:** a simple want and a happy ending; mild feelings that pass quickly; kind characters; short
words. No violence, fear, scary dark places, danger to children or animals, romance, weapons or
substances. Never name real studios, films or living artists in descriptions: describe looks in plain
words ("a small fox cub with a red scarf"). Sounds suggested should be friendly: birds, wind, footsteps.

**What fails and why:** the safety check refuses text it is unsure of and asks the owner. A "mild"
chase or a ghost in a cellar is refused. Rewrite the idea instead of arguing.
