# Shot length and story shape
> How long a shot should be and how a short children's film is built from shots. Unmeasured: from the first runs.

**When to use:** planning the shot list.

**How:** 3 to 6 seconds is typical, 2 to 8 is allowed. Start a scene with an establishing shot (the
place, characters small, a pan). Use closer, quieter shots for lines: one idea per shot, at most one
line (rarely two). End the film on a calm shot (a hold or a slow pull_out). A 15 second film is
3 to 5 shots; a 60 second film is 10 to 14. Shot seconds must add up to the target within 10 percent.

**What fails and why:** shots under 2 s cannot hold a motion and a line. A shot with three lines and
four motions is too much for ages 4 to 6. This note is a starting rule, not a measured one.
