# Expressions
> Which of the eleven expressions fits which feeling, and how to place them.

**When to use:** once per character at the start of a shot, and again where the feeling changes.

**How:** the set is fixed (see the capability list): neutral, happy, sad, surprised, grumpy, worried,
excited, shy, sleepy, proud, determined. Pick by the beat's feeling: discovery is surprised, then
happy or excited; letting something go is a calm happy; a wobble before relief is worried. Use
`set_expression` with a `start_s`; a change mid-shot should land just before the line or the motion
that shows it. Calm children's films stay mostly neutral, happy and curious (surprised).

**What fails and why:** changing expression every second looks twitchy. A face that contradicts its
line (sad face, cheerful words) confuses young viewers. The mouth during a line is driven by the
lip-sync, not by the expression, so choose an expression that suits the line's feeling.
